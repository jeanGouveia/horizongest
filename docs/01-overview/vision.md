# Vision

**HorizonGest Platform - Project Vision**

---

## Mission

HorizonGest is a multi-tenant SaaS platform designed to help restaurants manage their operations efficiently. Our mission is to provide a comprehensive, scalable, and user-friendly solution that enables restaurants to focus on what they do best: serving great food.

---

## Goals

### Primary Goals

1. **Efficiency:** Streamline restaurant operations
2. **Scalability:** Support growth from small to large chains
3. **Multi-tenancy:** Serve multiple restaurants from a single platform
4. **White-label:** Enable branding customization for different markets
5. **Reliability:** Ensure 99.9% uptime and data integrity

### Secondary Goals

1. **Integration:** Connect with payment gateways, delivery services
2. **Analytics:** Provide insights for business decisions
3. **Mobile:** Support mobile operations
4. **AI:** Leverage AI for recommendations and automation

---

## Target Audience
